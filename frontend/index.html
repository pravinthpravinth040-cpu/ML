How TrustCred AI Works

Four-stage empirical underwriting framework for micro-merchants with zero credit history.

1

UPI Flow Ingestion

Ingests 90 days of UPI transaction streams, segregating genuine customer payments (income), supplier expenses (costs), and inter-account transfers.

2

Circular Flow Audit

Identifies circular round-tripping transfers where accounts move funds back and forth to artificially inflate apparent revenue without real retail sales.

3

100-Point Scorecard

Evaluates 5 objective pillars: Income Stability (25), Repayment Capacity (30), Expense Discipline (15), Business Continuity (15), and Genuineness (15).

4

Underwriting Decision

Delivers plain-language explanations and debt service coverage bounds so micro-finance institutions can lend with audit-ready confidence.

Important Underwriting Scope & Limitations

Cash-Flow Evidence Score, Not a Bureau Prediction: TrustCred AI measures empirical velocity, revenue stability, and account legitimacy from transactional bank flows. It does not possess historical loan default records or willingness-to-pay credit bureau ratings, and does not claim to predict credit default behavior. With a 40-vendor demonstration cohort, statistical metrics are illustrative and intended for underwriting decision-support.
